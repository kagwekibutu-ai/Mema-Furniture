Quality Furniture, Made Accessible

Mema Furniture is a furniture brand specialising in the creation and supply of functional, stylish and quality furniture for modern homes and living spaces. Our range covers essential household furniture — sofas, beds, dining tables, baby cots, shelves and other customised solutions.

We believe furniture plays an important role in shaping the comfort and functionality of every home. Through attention to craftsmanship, customer preferences and dependable service, we aim to be a trusted furniture destination for customers in Murang'a and across Kenya.

Whether you are furnishing a new home, upgrading your living room or commissioning a custom design, our commitment is the same: quality furniture solutions, attentive service and convenient delivery.
